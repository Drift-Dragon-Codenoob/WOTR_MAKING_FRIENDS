# Synthesist appearance catalogue

1 November 2026. Development candidates, not accepted animation tests. David performs all in-game checks.

All 59 appearances are available to every mechanical base form, with or without purchased arms. The three groups organise browsing only. Some unusual shapes are grouped approximately. Your base form, evolutions and equipped items determine actual statistics and attacks; visible wings, arms, weapons and armour grant no powers.

Use existing model visuals only. Details already built into a prefab remain, while equipped-item overlays are suppressed on eidolon views. We do not copy or reconstruct a source NPC's separate inventory. Consequently, a built-in weapon may differ from your actual weapon, and some source NPC equipment may not appear at all. Dismissal should restore the protagonist's ordinary equipment display.

## Sources and persistence

The 57 existing Making Friends eidolon templates supply their prefabs only. Nabasu uses the native `CR12_NabasuAdvancedSummoned` blueprint and Gargoyle uses `CR4_GargoyleStandard`. Their statistics, inventory, feats, spells and subtype packages are not imported. Existing appearance keys and blueprint identifiers are preserved.

Creation, level-up and respec offer a group page followed by a separate model page. Preparation offers the same three groups without reopening evolution allocation. The saved appearance is used for fusion, preparation and Split Forms.

## Short player check

1. On a separate biped test, choose a Quadruped-group dragon. Confirm that points and mechanical form remain unchanged.
2. Enter preparation, finish required pet advancement, confirm the build and complete the one-minute fusion ritual. Check movement, casting and attack animations.
3. During preparation, try Nabasu or Gargoyle. Check that the selected look persists through fusion and save/load.
4. While equipped, confirm no item overlays appear on eidolon views, then dismiss. Check ordinary protagonist equipment rendering and another party member or pet.

Report invisible models, distorted size, missing animations, overlapping characters or equipment that stays hidden after dismissal. Compilation, reference checks and pure rules tests cannot establish these visual results.
